# Adopters

Organizations that use Lodia in some capacity. For what counts as an adopter,
see the [CNCF TOC FAQ](https://github.com/cncf/toc/blob/main/FAQ.md#what-is-the-definition-of-an-adopter).

| Organization | Type | Adoption level | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Segla](https://segla.io) | Service Provider | Evaluating | Hosted Kubernetes; evaluating Lodia for its customers' control planes. |

**Type:** End-User Organization (uses it internally), Service Provider (offers
it as part of a service), Project (integrates with it), or Consultancy.
**Adoption level:** Production, Dev/Test, Trialing or Evaluating.

## Adding your organization

Open a pull request that adds a row to the table. If your organization has to
stay anonymous, give its industry instead of its name.
